The Challenge
Why Astronauts Need Smarter Health Monitoring

Long-duration deep-space missions subject astronauts to extreme cognitive stress — sleep deprivation, isolation, prolonged EVA fatigue, and radiation effects on the brain.

Existing solutions either require continuous wearables that become impractical over months-long missions, or depend on real-time Earth communication — impossible during deep space travel where signals take up to 24 minutes each way.

Human error rooted in cognitive fatigue is a leading cause of near-misses in space operations. We need a system that works autonomously, silently, and continuously — without adding burden to the crew.

Operational Design
Two Seamless Operating Modes

Astronauts don't wear helmets continuously inside spacecraft. AURORA-X adapts automatically — no manual switching required.

🪖
HELMET MODE
DURING EVA
When the astronaut wears a compatible helmet with embedded EEG electrodes, AURORA-X gains full neuro-cognitive data on top of all spacecraft telemetry.
  • EEG electrodes in inner helmet lining
  • Frontal Theta — cognitive workload marker
  • Parietal Alpha — fatigue / relaxation marker
  • Motion & contact artifact filtering
  • QML model → cognitive state classification
  • All spacecraft sensors also active
🛸
CABIN MODE
INSIDE SPACECRAFT
Without a helmet, AURORA-X continues monitoring using existing medical telemetry, environmental sensors, sleep schedules, and mission workload data.
  • Heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen
  • Sleep duration & circadian rhythm
  • Cabin CO₂, pressure, radiation, light
  • Task load & mission phase tracking
  • Mood check-ins when data gaps detected
  • No EEG — intelligent fallback active
